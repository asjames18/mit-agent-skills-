# Copyright practices for published content

Standing practices for anything published on Antonio's sites and channels. Practice, not legal advice — real copyright questions go to a lawyer.

## Text
- Write original copy. Never reproduce someone else's article, post, or creative work at length.
- Short attributed quotes only (a line or two). Link to the source instead of copying it.
- Every quote gets an attribution line (— Name, Source) or it gets removed.
- As a screening rule, verbatim quotes over ~90 words get trimmed, paraphrased, or cut.

## Song lyrics
- Assume lyrics are enforced. Music publishers pursue lyric reproductions aggressively.
- Default: reference the song title only.
- At most a one-line snippet with attribution, and only when it genuinely serves the piece.

## Scripture
- Public-domain translations (KJV, WEB, ASV, YLT) are fine for extended quotation.
- Copyrighted translations (NIV, ESV, NKJV, NLT, NASB, CSB, etc.) only in brief snippets with attribution.
- Screening rule: quoted scripture over ~50 words should be KJV/WEB, or trimmed to a brief modern-translation excerpt.

## Images and media
- Only owned, licensed, or AI-generated originals.
- Never hotlink or scrape images from search results or other sites.
- Known free-license libraries (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Wikimedia, Openverse) are acceptable with their license terms honored; keep track of the source.

## Process
- Run `bin/copyright-scan` on every draft before it is queued or sent for publish approval.
- FLAG findings must be fixed or explicitly accepted by Antonio. The scanner never clears content on its own.
- Nothing publishes without Antonio's explicit approval for that piece.
