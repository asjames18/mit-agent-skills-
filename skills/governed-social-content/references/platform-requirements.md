# Platform Requirements

Last researched against official platform sources: **2026-09-18**.

Use this reference to plan assets, then recheck the exact publishing surface during the current task. Platform limits vary by account tier, region, device, native app, web composer, API, scheduler, business suite, and advertising product.

## Requirements protocol

Before calling any item approval-ready:

1. Identify the platform, account type, content format, and publishing surface.
2. Open the current official help or developer documentation for that exact surface.
3. Verify text length, media count, file type, file size, duration, dimensions, aspect ratio, frame rate, caption or subtitle support, audience controls, link behavior, and scheduling support that apply.
4. Check accessibility, copyright, music, branded-content, sponsorship, privacy, and disclosure requirements.
5. Record the source URL and date checked in the draft packet.
6. Validate the final asset in the actual composer or API before approval. A remembered or previously recorded limit is not action-time verification.

If official sources conflict, prefer the source for the exact publishing surface and verify in the active composer. Mark unresolved constraints as `NEEDS CONFIRMATION`; do not claim the asset is publish-ready.

## X

Verified from official X Help on 2026-09-18:

- A standard post supports up to **280 characters**.
- A post can contain up to **4 total media items**. A single GIF cannot be combined as an animated GIF with multiple images.
- Longer posts require Premium. X's official post-types page describes longer posts up to **25,000 characters**, but availability and client behavior must be verified for the active account and composer.
- Photos can be up to **5 MB**. Animated GIFs can be up to **5 MB on mobile** and **15 MB on web**. Supported image types listed by X are GIF, JPEG, and PNG.
- For non-Premium video uploads, X documents up to **140 seconds** and **512 MB**. Premium upload allowances are much larger and depend on resolution and duration.
- X documents web-video resolution, aspect-ratio, frame-rate, and bitrate limits separately; recheck them for each asset.
- Threads are ordered connected posts. Validate every post and the complete order, not only the combined copy.

Official sources:

- [Post photos or GIFs](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/posting-gifs-and-pictures)
- [Share videos on X](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-videos)

## LinkedIn

Verified from official LinkedIn Help on 2026-09-18:

- A standard LinkedIn post supports up to **3,000 characters**. LinkedIn directs longer material toward its article publishing surface.
- Organic video uploads support a maximum file size of **5 GB** and maximum duration of **15 minutes**. Minimum duration differs between desktop and mobile.
- Organic video requirements include a documented resolution range, aspect-ratio range, frame-rate range, bitrate range, and safe-zone guidance. Recheck these values against the active upload surface.
- A document post accepts **one document**, requires a title, supports listed office-document and PDF formats, and is limited to **100 MB** and **300 pages**.
- Personal-profile, Page, Career Page, organic, and ad video requirements can differ. Use the documentation matching the actual publisher and content type.
- Visibility and comment controls are part of the post configuration and must be included in the action-ready review.

Official sources:

- [Post and share updates](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a528176)
- [Share videos on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a7486279)
- [Document uploads FAQ](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a523054/document-uploads-on-linkedin-faq)

## Facebook

Verified from official Meta Help on 2026-09-18:

- Facebook feed publishing supports text and multiple content types such as photos, videos, GIFs, and live video.
- The audience selector is an explicit posting control. Profile, Page, group, and reel audiences are not interchangeable.
- Facebook supports uploaded captions for video, including SubRip (`.srt`) files on documented surfaces.
- Meta's help content reflects an active product transition in which Facebook videos are becoming reels. Some official pages still describe older reel-duration behavior while newer guidance says videos may become reels regardless of length or orientation.
- Because of that transition, do not hard-code one Facebook reel-duration limit. Verify the current composer, account, and region before approving a video.
- Instagram-to-Facebook sharing may reach a different audience. Verify the Facebook audience independently before any cross-post.

The exact feed-text cap, media-count cap, reel duration, and upload specifications were not treated as confirmed universal limits in this review because official behavior varies by surface and is changing. Verify them live.

## Instagram

Verified from official Meta sources available on 2026-09-18:

- Instagram content can use feed images, carousels, reels, and stories, but their limits and publishing capabilities differ.
- Meta recommends Reels creative built vertically at **9:16**, with audio and important elements inside safe zones. Treat that as creative guidance, then verify the actual organic or API upload requirements.
- Meta's official Instagram API collection documents server-published Reels using MOV or MP4 containers, AAC audio, H.264 or HEVC video, **23–60 FPS**, recommended **9:16**, **3 seconds to 15 minutes**, and up to **1 GB**. These are API-path requirements and must not be assumed to describe every native-app flow.
- Copyright, music licensing, branded-content labels, collaboration permissions, and disclosure requirements must be checked before approval.
- Carousel item count, caption length, hashtag and mention caps, native Reel duration, story limits, image ratios, and feature availability must be rechecked in current official help or the active composer. Do not rely on third-party limit tables as final authority.

Official sources:

- [Meta Reels creative](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads)
- [Meta Instagram API collection — Reels Publishing](https://www.postman.com/meta/instagram/folder/f95kq5e/reels-publishing)

## TikTok

Verified from official TikTok support and TikTok for Business on 2026-09-18:

- TikTok supports video, photo, and text posts. Available controls differ between the app and browser.
- TikTok Studio web uploads document MP4 or WebM, resolution of **720 × 1280 or higher**, duration up to **30 minutes**, and file size below **10 GB**.
- The publishing screen can include a caption, cover, audience selection, comment controls, Duet and Stitch permissions, and a copyright check. These settings belong in action-time review.
- TikTok-first creative guidance favors vertical **9:16**, high-resolution, safe-zone-aware video with a hook, body, close, sound, and on-screen captions or text.
- App capture duration, photo-post count, caption limits, link availability, music rights, and regional features can differ. Verify them in the active account and composer.

Official sources:

- [TikTok creator tools and Studio upload requirements](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/creator-tools-on-tiktok)
- [TikTok editing, posting, and deleting](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/editing-posting-and-deleting)
- [TikTok Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/creative-codes)

## Approval-packet requirement

Add a `Platform requirements` section to every approval-ready packet:

- Publishing surface and account type
- Official sources consulted
- Date checked
- Text count
- Media count and order
- File type and file size
- Dimensions, aspect ratio, and safe-zone result
- Duration, frame rate, bitrate, and audio result when relevant
- Captions, subtitles, alt text, and cover
- Audience, visibility, comment, remix, Duet, Stitch, or collaboration settings
- Link, mention, hashtag, disclosure, branded-content, copyright, and music checks
- Scheduling or API constraints
- Result: `PASS`, `NEEDS CONFIRMATION`, or `NOT APPLICABLE`
