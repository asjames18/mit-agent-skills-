# Output Templates

Use only the template relevant to the current task. Do not create empty records merely to fill a workflow.

## Content capture card

- What happened?
- What was built, fixed, learned, tested, rejected, observed, or decided?
- Why might one reader care?
- Supporting artifact or evidence
- Confirmed facts
- Missing confirmation
- Safe-to-share summary
- Confidentiality or disclosure risks
- Possible primary reader
- Possible platform

## Source and claim card

- Claim
- Source owner and URL
- Source type
- Event or effective date
- Publication or update date
- Date checked
- Claim classification
- Time-sensitive?
- Contradicting or qualifying evidence
- Safe wording
- Confidence

## Approval-ready draft packet

- Decision: `DRAFT RECOMMENDED` or `NEEDS CONFIRMATION`
- Platform and channel account
- Primary reader and objective
- Content lane or pillar
- Claim state
- Evidence and source provenance
- Exact proposed copy
- Media recommendation and immutable media identifier when relevant
- Accuracy, freshness, privacy, disclosure, and repetition checks
- Open questions or owner confirmations
- Content item ID, revision ID, and content hash when stable
- Intended action, ordering, and targets
- Status: `draft` or `needs confirmation`
- Next safe action

## No-post decision

- Decision: `NO POST RECOMMENDED`
- Reason
- Evidence or input that is missing
- Risk avoided
- Smallest real input that could unlock a useful post

## Published-content learning card

- Published revision and public URL
- Platform and publication time
- Intended reader and outcome
- Available early and later signals
- Qualified outcome
- What likely contributed
- What remains unknown
- Recommendation: reuse, revise, retest, or retire
